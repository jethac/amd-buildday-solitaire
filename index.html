How to play

Build the foundations

Move every card to its matching suit foundation, building each one from Ace to King. You win when all four foundations are complete.

Work the tableau

Build downward in alternating colors. Move any correctly ordered face-up sequence. Only a King or a King-led sequence can fill an empty column. Revealing a hidden card turns it over automatically.

Use the stock

Click the stock to draw one or three cards, depending on your mode. Only the top waste card is playable. When the stock is empty, click its recycle symbol to turn the waste over without shuffling.

Controls

Mouse / touch
Drag, or select then choose a destination. Double-click or double-tap for foundations.
Arrow keys
Move between piles; Up/Down chooses a tableau card.
Enter / Space
Select, drop, or draw from the stock.
F / H / U
Foundation / Hint / Undo. Ctrl or Cmd + Z also undoes.
Escape
Cancel a selection or close a dialog.

Scoring

Waste to tableau: +5. Waste or tableau to foundation: +10. Reveal a tableau card: +5. Foundation back to tableau: −15. The score never falls below zero. Undo reverses the score change.
